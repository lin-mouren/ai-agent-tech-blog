# Introducing Anthropic Interviewer: What 1,250 professionals told us about working with AI

December 4, 2025

We're launching a new tool, Anthropic Interviewer, to help understand people's perspectives on AI. In this research post, we introduce the tool, describe a test of it on a sample of professionals, and discuss our early findings.

## Introduction

Millions of people now use AI every day. As a company developing AI systems, we want to know how and why they're doing so, and how it affects them. We built an interview tool called Anthropic Interviewer. Powered by Claude, Anthropic Interviewer runs detailed interviews automatically at unprecedented scale.

To test Anthropic Interviewer, we had it run 1,250 interviews with professionals—the general workforce (N=1,000), scientists (N=125), and creatives (N=125)—about their views on AI.

## Key Findings

### AI's impact in the general workforce
86% of professionals reported that AI saves them time and 65% said they were satisfied with the role AI plays in their work. 69% of professionals mentioned the social stigma that can come with using AI tools at work. While 41% felt secure in their work, 55% expressed anxiety about AI's impact on their future.

### Augmentation versus automation
65% of participants described AI's primary role as augmentative; 35% described it as automative. This differed from actual Claude usage data, which showed 47% augmentation and 49% automation, suggesting people may perceive their AI use as more collaborative than it actually is.

### AI's impact on creative professions
97% of creatives reported that AI saved them time and 68% said it increased their work's quality. However, 70% mentioned managing peer judgment around AI use. Economic anxiety was pervasive, with voice actors noting certain sectors have "essentially died due to the rise of AI."

### AI's impact on scientific work
Scientists primarily reported using AI for literature review, coding, and writing—not for core research tasks. Trust and reliability concerns were the primary barrier in 79% of interviews. 91% of scientists expressed a desire for more AI assistance in their research.

## Method

Anthropic Interviewer operates in three stages: planning, interviewing, and analysis. It uses Claude to create adaptive, real-time interviews that last about 10-15 minutes per participant. Interviews were conducted on Claude.ai with consent from all participants.
