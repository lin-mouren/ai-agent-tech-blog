# Claude's new constitution

Jan 22, 2026 | Announcements

We are publishing a new constitution for our AI model, Claude. It is a detailed description of Anthropic's vision for Claude's values and behavior; a holistic document that explains the context in which Claude operates and the kind of entity we would like Claude to be.

The constitution is a crucial part of our model training process, and its content directly shapes Claude's behavior. We think that the way the new constitution is written—with a thorough explanation of our intentions and the reasons behind them—makes it more likely to cultivate good values during training.

## What is Claude's Constitution?

Claude's constitution is the foundational document that both expresses and shapes who Claude is. It contains detailed explanations of the values we would like Claude to embody and the reasons why. The constitution gives Claude information about its situation and offers advice for how to deal with difficult situations and tradeoffs, like balancing honesty with compassion and the protection of sensitive information.

## Our new approach

Our previous constitution was composed of a list of standalone principles. We have come to believe that a different approach is necessary. AI models like Claude need to understand why we want them to behave in certain ways, and we need to explain this to them rather than merely specify what we want them to do.

## A brief summary of the new constitution

We want all current Claude models to be:
1. **Broadly safe**: not undermining appropriate human mechanisms to oversee AI
2. **Broadly ethical**: being honest, acting according to good values
3. **Compliant with Anthropic's guidelines**: acting in accordance with specific guidelines
4. **Genuinely helpful**: benefiting the operators and users they interact with

The main sections cover: Helpfulness, Anthropic's guidelines, Claude's ethics, Being broadly safe, and Claude's nature. We are releasing the full text of the constitution today under a Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Deed, meaning it can be freely used by anyone for any purpose.
