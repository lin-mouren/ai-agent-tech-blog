# Claude for Life Sciences

Increasing the rate of scientific progress is a core part of Anthropic's public benefit mission.

We are focused on building the tools to allow researchers to make new discoveries. To do this, we're rolling out several improvements that aim to make Claude a better partner for those who work in the life sciences.

## Making Claude a better research partner

Claude Sonnet 4.5 is significantly better than previous models at a range of life sciences tasks. On Protocol QA, a benchmark that tests understanding of laboratory protocols, Sonnet 4.5 scores 0.83, against a human baseline of 0.79.

To make Claude more useful for scientific work, we're now adding several new connectors to scientific platforms, the ability to use Agent Skills, and life sciences-specific support.

## Connecting Claude to scientific tools

New connectors include Benchling, BioRender, PubMed, Scholar Gateway (Wiley), Synapse.org, and 10x Genomics.

## Developing skills for Claude

We released Agent Skills, folders including instructions, scripts, and resources that Claude can use to improve how it performs specific tasks. We're developing scientific skills for Claude, beginning with single-cell-rna-qc.

## Partnerships and customers

We're partnering with companies including Sanofi, Benchling, Broad Institute, Novo Nordisk, Stanford University, and PwC. Many of these organizations are already using Claude for real-world scientific tasks.

## Getting started

Claude for Life Sciences is available through Claude.com and on the AWS Marketplace.
