# Claude and your productivity platforms

Claude now integrates with Microsoft 365 and offers enterprise search across your connected tools.

Claude works with the productivity tools you use every day, bringing context from your documents, emails, and calendar directly into your conversations.

## Microsoft 365 connector for Claude

Connect Claude with Microsoft 365 using our MCP connector. Claude works with:

- **Microsoft SharePoint and OneDrive**: Search and analyze documents across sites and libraries.
- **Microsoft Outlook**: Access email threads, analyze communications patterns.
- **Microsoft Teams**: Search through chat conversations, channel discussions, and meeting summaries.

## Enterprise search

We're making it easier to access company knowledge through a dedicated, shared project. When you ask Claude a question, it can search across your company's connected data sources in one place.

Enterprise search is particularly valuable for onboarding new team members, answering strategic questions, and quickly identifying the right internal experts.

## Getting started

The Microsoft 365 connector is available today for all Claude Team and Enterprise plan customers. Enterprise search is enabled for all Team and Enterprise organizations.
