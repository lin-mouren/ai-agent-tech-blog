## Executive Summary

The August 2025 – May 2026 period represents a pivotal 10-month stretch in AI development. GPT-5 and Claude 4 launched as true frontier models. Google DeepMind advanced on multiple fronts — from scientific reasoning to robotics. Meta underwent four organizational restructurings, acquired Scale AI for $14.3B, and pivoted from open-source Llama to proprietary Muse Spark.

Key themes:
- **Agentic AI** emerged as the primary development focus across all vendors
- **Context windows** expanded dramatically (GPT-5.4: 1M, Llama 4 Scout: 10M tokens)
- **Hardware investment** reached unprecedented scale (Meta: $115-135B capex for 2026)
- **Open source vs. closed source** tensions intensified (Meta pivoted with Muse Spark)
- **SWE-bench** became the primary coding benchmark battleground

## Key Technology Themes 2025–2026

### 1. Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) Everywhere
Llama 4 Scout/Maverick, Grok 3, and multiple proprietary models adopted MoE for compute efficiency. MoE allows massive total parameter counts with fewer active parameters per inference.

### 2. Agentic AI as Primary Use Case
All four vendors shifted primary marketing from "chat" to "agent" framing. SWE-bench emerged as the dominant benchmark because it measures real-world software engineering tasks that agents perform.

### 3. Hardware Differentiation
Meta (MTIA chips), Google (TPUs), Amazon (Trainium/Inferentia) all accelerated custom silicon programs to reduce NVIDIA dependence. 6-month chip cadences vs. traditional 2-year cycles.

### 4. Safety + Security as Strategy
- Anthropic: Project Glasswing (AI for security vulnerability discovery), NSC collaboration
- OpenAI: Enhanced safety system cards, government partnerships
- Meta: Llama Defenders Program, safety evaluations for Muse Spark

### 5. Multimodal Convergence
Every major model now handles text + image + audio as baseline. Video understanding (V-JEPA 2, Gemini 2.5) and audio separation (SAM Audio) represent the next frontier.

### 6. World Models Emerging
Meta's V-JEPA 2 and Google's work on physical world understanding point toward AI that can reason about physical reality, not just language. This is the prerequisite for reliable robotics.

## Conclusion

August 2025 to May 2026 will be remembered as the period when:
1. **Frontier AI became multimodal by default** (text-only models are now niche)
2. **Agentic capabilities moved from demos to production** (SWE-bench scores matter for real deployments)
3. **Meta made its most consequential AI bet** ($14.3B Scale AI + Muse Spark pivot)
4. **Hardware investment reached sovereign-scale** (Meta at $115-135B capex for 2026)
5. **World models emerged as the next research frontier** (V-JEPA 2 pointing toward physical AI)

The race entering mid-2026 is more competitive, more expensive, and more strategically complex than at any previous point. All four vendors have credible paths to frontier capability, but different bets on architecture, openness, and deployment strategy.
