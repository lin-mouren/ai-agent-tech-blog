# GPT-5.3 Instant: Smoother, more useful everyday conversations

March 3, 2026

Today, we are releasing an update to ChatGPT's most-used model that makes everyday conversations more consistently helpful and fluid. GPT-5.3 Instant delivers more accurate answers, richer and better-contextualized results when searching the web, and reduces unnecessary dead ends, caveats, and overly declarative phrasing.

This update focuses on the parts of the ChatGPT experience people feel every day: tone, relevance, and conversational flow. These are nuanced problems that do not always show up in benchmarks, but shape whether ChatGPT feels helpful or frustrating.

## Better judgment around refusals and fewer disclaimers

GPT-5.3 Instant significantly reduces unnecessary refusals, while toning down overly defensive or moralizing preambles before answering the question. When a useful answer is appropriate, the model should not hedge or refuse.

## More useful, well-synthesized answers when using search

When the model uses web search, GPT-5.3 Instant improves the quality of answers. It more effectively balances what it finds online with its own knowledge and reasoning - using its existing understanding to contextualize recent news rather than just summarizing search results.

## More reliably accurate responses

GPT-5.3 Instant demonstrates improved accuracy on internal evaluations. It is available starting today to all users in ChatGPT, as well as to developers in the API as 'gpt-5.3-chat-latest'. GPT-5.2 Instant will remain available for three months for paid users under Legacy Models before retirement on June 3, 2026.
