# Meta Ray-Ban Display AI Glasses: Launch and 2025-2026 AI Feature Roadmap

**Date:** September 30, 2025 (launch) through May 2026  
**Vendor:** Meta  
**Source:** https://www.meta.com/blog/meta-ray-ban-display-ai-glasses-connect-2025/

## Launch (September 30, 2025)

Meta launched Ray-Ban Display AI Glasses — the first Meta glasses with an in-lens display. Priced at $799 USD (includes Meta Neural Band EMG wristband). Available at Best Buy, LensCrafters, Sunglass Hut, Ray-Ban Stores in the US.

### Key Features at Launch
- **In-lens monocular display:** Shows answers, navigation, step-by-step instructions, notifications
- **Meta Neural Band:** EMG (electromyography) wristband that interprets muscle signals for hands-free gesture control
- **Meta AI with Visuals:** Shows visual answers instead of just reading them back
- **Music:** Amazon Music, Apple Music, Spotify with visual metadata display
- **Multimodal Live AI:** See what you see, ask questions in real-time

## Software Updates Through May 2026

### November-December 2025 (v20-21)
- Expanded to Canada, France, Italy, UK
- Enhanced Live AI with persistent sessions
- New voice interaction improvements

### March 2026 (v23-24)
- **Prescription-Optimized Versions:** Ray-Ban Meta Blayzer Optics (Gen 2) and Scriber Optics (Gen 2) launched ($499, available April 14) supporting nearly all prescriptions
- **Hands-free nutrition tracking:** Voice or photo to log meals, personalized insights over time
- **Neural Handwriting:** Write with finger on any surface to reply to messages silently
- **WhatsApp summaries and recall by Meta AI**

### May 2026 (v5)
- **AI History and AI Home:** Pick up recent AI conversations, quick shortcuts visible on display
- Faster voice responses powered by Muse Spark
- Smarter context-aware assistance
- Shopping and recommendations integrated
- Real-time language translation

## Hardware Roadmap

- 2025: Ray-Ban Display (monocular HUD) + Oakley Meta (smart glasses)
- 2026: Aperol & Bellini (next-gen mainstream, face recognition capability being explored)
- 2027+: Binocular HUD and true AR glasses

## Business Metrics

- Ray-Ban Meta sales tripled in 2025 vs. 2024
- Hundreds of millions of Meta Neural Band units planned
- Meta planning 20 million unit production ramp for Ray-Ban AI glasses in 2026
