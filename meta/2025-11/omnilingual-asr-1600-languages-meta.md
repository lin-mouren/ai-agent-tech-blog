# Omnilingual ASR: Automatic Speech Recognition for 1,600+ Languages

**Date:** November 10, 2025  
**Vendor:** Meta (FAIR)  
**Source:** https://ai.meta.com/blog/omnilingual-asr-advancing-automatic-speech-recognition/

## Overview

Meta's FAIR team introduced Omnilingual ASR, a groundbreaking suite of speech recognition models supporting over 1,600 languages — including 500 low-resource languages never before transcribed by AI. The system uses a community-driven framework enabling anyone to extend coverage to new languages with just a few personal voice samples.

## Key Releases

1. **Omnilingual ASR Models:** State-of-the-art automatic speech recognition for 1,600+ languages
2. **Omnilingual wav2vec 2.0:** Scaled-up massively multilingual speech representation model (7B parameters) for downstream speech tasks
3. **Omnilingual ASR Corpus:** Transcribed speech collection in 350 underserved languages, curated with global partners

## Model Sizes

- Lightweight 300M parameter models: Designed for low-power/edge devices
- 7B parameter models: Maximum accuracy for high-performance use cases

## Community-Driven Extension

The framework is designed so anyone can extend Omnilingual ASR to new languages using just a few personal speech samples — making it accessible for endangered language documentation and low-resource language communities worldwide.

## Significance

Omnilingual ASR addresses a critical gap: the vast majority of AI speech technology works in fewer than 50 languages, leaving billions of speakers behind. By covering 1,600+ languages, Meta positions this as a foundational technology for global accessibility, scientific preservation of endangered languages, and enabling AI assistants in every language community.
