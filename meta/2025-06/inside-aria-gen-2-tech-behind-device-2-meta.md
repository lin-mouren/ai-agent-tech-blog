# Inside Aria Gen 2: Explore the Cutting-Edge Tech Behind the Device

June 4, 2025

Earlier this year, we announced our latest research glasses, Aria Gen 2, marking the continuation of Project Aria's mission to enable researchers worldwide to advance machine perception, contextual AI, and robotics. Today, we're sharing more about the technology inside Aria Gen 2.

Aria Gen 2 is a wearable device combining the latest advancements in computer vision, machine learning, and sensor technology. The compact form factor and lightweight design (74-76g) make it ideal for researchers, with eight size variations to accommodate different face morphologies.

## Key Advancements

### Computer Vision Camera Enhancements
- **High Dynamic Range (HDR):** 120 dB range (vs 70 dB in Gen 1) for superior CV across diverse lighting conditions;
- **Wide Field of View:** Four CV cameras (doubling Gen 1) for advanced 3D hand and object tracking;
- **Stereo Overlap:** Increased to 80 degrees (from 35 degrees) for stereo-based foundation models.

### New Sensor Integrations
- **Ambient Light Sensor (ALS):** Calibrated ALS with ultraviolet mode to distinguish indoor/outdoor lighting;
- **Contact Microphone:** Embedded in nosepad for enhanced audio capture in noisy environments;
- **Heart Rate Sensor:** Photoplethysmography (PPG) sensor for heart rate estimation.

### Device Time Alignment
Onboard Sub-GHz radio technology enables precise time alignment with sub-millisecond accuracy across multiple Aria Gen 2 devices.

### On-device Machine Perception
Running on Meta's energy-efficient custom coprocessor:
- Visual Inertial Odometry (VIO) for 6DOF tracking;
- Camera-based eye tracking (gaze, pupil diameter, blink detection);
- Hand tracking for articulated hand-joint poses in 3D.

## The Future of Aria

Aria Gen 2 glasses pave the way for innovations that will define the next computing platform. Applications for Aria Gen 2 will open later this year.
