# Meta Invests $14.3 Billion in Scale AI, Brings Alexandr Wang In to Lead Superintelligence Labs

**Date:** June 12, 2025  
**Vendor:** Meta  
**Source:** https://scale.com/blog/scale-ai-announces-next-phase-of-company-evolution

## Overview

In one of the largest strategic AI deals of 2025, Meta announced a $14.3 billion investment to acquire a 49% stake in Scale AI, the leading AI data labeling and evaluation company. Scale AI founder and CEO Alexandr Wang (28 years old at time of deal) stepped down as CEO to join Meta as Chief AI Officer, leading a newly formed Meta Superintelligence Labs division.

## Deal Structure

- **Investment:** $14.3 billion for 49% stake in Scale AI (post-money valuation: ~$29 billion)
- **Voting Rights:** Meta transfers its voting rights to Wang
- **Proceeds:** Distributed as dividend to Scale shareholders (substantial liquidity event)
- **Scale Independence:** Scale AI remains operationally independent; Jason Droege (strategy chief) becomes CEO
- **Wang Role:** Joins Meta as Chief AI Officer, continues as Scale board director

## Strategic Rationale

### For Meta
- Access to Scale AI's high-quality data labeling infrastructure spanning text, images, video, and audio
- Brings in Wang's expertise in AI data and evaluation
- Wang's Washington DC connections for potential government/defense AI contracts
- Narrative reset after lukewarm Llama 4 reception

### Market Disruption
- OpenAI and Google began winding down Scale AI partnerships after the deal
- Scale's client diversification (previously served all major AI labs) severely disrupted
- Industry concern: Meta potentially gains insight into how rivals train their models

## Industry Reaction

The deal was widely described as the inflection point where AI collaboration ended and serious strategic competition began. Scale AI's role as neutral infrastructure provider for the entire AI industry was fundamentally altered. Some insiders compared it to the Microsoft-OpenAI relationship, others to Microsoft-Inflection (talent acquisition).
