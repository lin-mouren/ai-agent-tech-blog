# Introducing Muse Spark: Meta Superintelligence Labs' First Model

**Date:** April 8, 2026  
**Vendor:** Meta (Meta Superintelligence Labs)  
**Source:** https://ai.meta.com/blog/introducing-muse-spark-msl

## Overview

Meta debuted Muse Spark — its most powerful AI model yet and the first major product from Chief AI Officer Alexandr Wang's Meta Superintelligence Labs. Originally codenamed "Avocado," Muse Spark marks a fundamental shift: it is a closed, proprietary model (not open-source like Llama), natively multimodal, and designed specifically for personal superintelligence — an assistant that understands each user's history, interests, content, and relationships.

## Key Capabilities

- **Natively Multimodal Reasoning:** Processes and reasons across text, images, and video
- **Visual Chain of Thought:** Reasoning shown step-by-step through visual modalities
- **Tool Use:** Can use external tools and APIs
- **Multi-Agent Orchestration:** Coordinates multiple AI agents for complex tasks
- **Health, Science, Math:** Competitive performance in scientific reasoning

## Strategic Significance

- **First closed-source flagship from Meta:** Historic shift from open-source Llama strategy
- **Scaling trajectory:** Muse Spark is the "early data point" on Meta's scaling ladder; larger Muse models are in active development
- **Personal superintelligence:** Designed to power assistant that cites your Instagram posts, Facebook content, and Threads activity for personalized answers
- **Ground-up overhaul:** Built on completely rebuilt research and infrastructure (Hyperion data center)
- **Better with less compute:** Equivalent capability to mid-size Llama 4 for "an order of magnitude less compute" via improved training techniques

## Deployment

- **Available:** meta.ai and Meta AI app at launch
- **Rolling out:** WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, Messenger, AI glasses over weeks following
- **API:** Private preview with select partners; public API delayed (WSJ reported repeated pushbacks as of early June 2026)
- **EU Availability:** Rolling out with regulatory considerations

## May 2026 Update (May 12)

- Faster voice responses
- Smarter AI glasses
- New shopping and product recommendations in conversations
- Voice conversations with natural interruption, topic switching, language swapping
- Can generate images and pull Reels/maps recommendations during voice chat

## Business Context

- Meta stock rose 9% on announcement day
- Meta AI reached 1 billion monthly active users (May 2025)
- Meta's 2026 AI capex: $115-135 billion
- Muse Spark comes ~9 months after Wang joined from Scale AI

## Criticism

- Consumer-first focus (vs. enterprise focus of OpenAI/Anthropic)
- Digital privacy concerns: personalization requires extensive user data
- API delays frustrated developer community
