# Meta Compute: Decade-Long AI Infrastructure Initiative

**Date:** January 13, 2026  
**Vendor:** Meta  
**Source:** MediaPost / Meta Threads announcement

## Overview

Mark Zuckerberg announced "Meta Compute" via a post on Threads — a new top-level initiative mapping out Meta's decade-long plan to develop global AI infrastructure. The initiative positions Meta as a builder of "tens of gigawatts this decade, and hundreds of gigawatts or more over time."

## Key Elements

### Leadership
- **Santosh Janardhan** (former Google exec, current head of global infrastructure and engineering at Meta)
- **Daniel Gross** (former CEO/co-founder of Safe Superintelligence)

### Infrastructure Investments Announced
- **$14.3B Scale AI stake** (49%, announced June 2025) — providing data quality infrastructure
- **$27B financing deal with Blue Owl Capital** to pay for the Hyperion multi-gigawatt data center
- **$1.5B data center in El Paso, Texas**
- **2026 AI capex guidance:** $115-135 billion (nearly twice 2025)

### Hyperion Data Center
- Multi-gigawatt facility — one of the largest planned AI data centers in the world
- Financed through Blue Owl Capital deal
- Purpose: Support Muse Spark training and future Muse model development

## Economic Impact Claims

- Over 30,000 skilled trade jobs supported by Meta data center projects over 15 years
- 5,000 operational jobs created
- Positioned as strategic economic infrastructure for the United States

## Strategic Vision

Meta Compute represents the full-stack infrastructure bet to enable personal superintelligence: if Muse Spark is the model, Hyperion is the power source. Zuckerberg's framing of "hundreds of gigawatts" puts Meta's ambitions on a scale rivaling national power grids.
