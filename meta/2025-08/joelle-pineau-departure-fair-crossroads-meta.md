# Meta's Head of AI Research Joelle Pineau Announces Departure

**Date:** April 1, 2025 (announcement) / May 30, 2025 (last day)  
**Vendor:** Meta  
**Source:** https://www.cnbc.com/2025/04/01/metas-head-of-ai-research-announces-departure.html

## Overview

Joelle Pineau, Meta's VP of AI Research and head of FAIR (Fundamental AI Research), announced her departure via LinkedIn on April 1, 2025. Her last day was May 30, 2025. Pineau had led FAIR since 2023 and joined Meta in 2017 to found their Montreal AI research lab.

## Context and Timing

The departure was notable for multiple reasons:
1. **Llama 4 controversy:** Came just days after the April 5 Llama 4 launch that attracted criticism for benchmark irregularities and performance gaps vs. competitors
2. **DeepSeek panic:** In February 2025, an anonymous Meta employee on Blind described the "Meta GenAI Org in Panic Mode" over DeepSeek's competitive performance
3. **AGI race acceleration:** Meta was pouring billions into AI, but FAIR's academic research mission seemed misaligned with product urgency
4. **Scale AI deal coming:** The $14.3B Scale AI deal with Alexandr Wang would be announced 11 weeks later, fundamentally reshaping Meta's AI leadership

## FAIR's Legacy Under Pineau

During her tenure, FAIR produced or maintained: PyTorch, FAISS, RoBERTa, DINOv2, Llama series, SAM, Codegen, Audiobox, and many others.

## What Came Next

- Robert Fergus (returned from DeepMind) took over FAIR leadership
- Meta's AI restructuring (4th in 6 months) in August 2025 separated FAIR (long-term research) from TBD Lab (product-focused)
- Some insiders described FAIR as "dying a slow death"; Yann LeCun called it "a new beginning" focused on Advanced Machine Intelligence
