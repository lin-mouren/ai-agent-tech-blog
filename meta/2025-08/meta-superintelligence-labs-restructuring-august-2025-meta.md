# Meta Creates Superintelligence Labs: Fourth AI Restructuring in Six Months

**Date:** August 20, 2025  
**Vendor:** Meta  
**Source:** Bloomberg / ainews.com

## Overview

Meta formally launched Meta Superintelligence Labs (MSL), its fourth AI restructuring in six months. Chief AI Officer Alexandr Wang — who joined Meta in June 2025 as part of the $14.3B Scale AI deal — sent an internal memo announcing the reorganization into four distinct teams designed to "accelerate" the pursuit of superintelligence.

## Four MSL Teams

1. **TBD Lab** (led by Alexandr Wang): Foundation model research, including Llama tooling and Meta's AI assistant
2. **FAIR (Fundamental AI Research):** Long-term foundational research and basic science; led by Robert Fergus (returned from DeepMind)
3. **Applied Research & Products:** Productization of AI capabilities across Meta's apps
4. **MSL Infra** (led by Aparna Ramani): Infrastructure for AI training and inference at scale

## Key Personnel Changes

- **Alexandr Wang** (Chief AI Officer): Leading TBD Lab, overseeing all of MSL
- **Robert Fergus:** Returning from DeepMind to lead FAIR
- **Ahmad Al-Dahle and Amir Frenkel:** AGI foundations group dissolved; moving to strategic MSL initiatives
- **Connor Hayes:** Previously headed AI products group; reassigned to run Threads
- **Loredana Crisan:** Departing Meta for Figma

## Context

This restructuring came after:
- The April 2025 departure of FAIR head Joelle Pineau (left May 30, 2025)
- Mixed reception of Llama 4 models (released April 5, 2025) amid benchmark controversy
- The $14.3B Scale AI investment (June 12, 2025) bringing Alexandr Wang to Meta
- Growing competitive pressure from OpenAI and Google DeepMind

## Strategic Direction

Zuckerberg's stated goal: build AI that can autonomously perform cognitive tasks at superhuman level. Meta's AI infrastructure spending planned at $60-65B in 2025, ramping significantly in 2026. The separation between short-term product AI (TBD Lab) and long-term research (FAIR) mirrors how DeepMind and OpenAI have structured their own research arms.
