# LlamaCon 2025: Meta's First Generative AI Developer Conference

**Date:** April 29, 2025  
**Vendor:** Meta  
**Source:** https://ai.meta.com/blog/llamacon-llama-news

## Overview

On April 29, 2025, Meta held LlamaCon at its Menlo Park HQ — its first developer conference dedicated to generative AI and the Llama open-source ecosystem. The event was livestreamed worldwide and featured keynotes from CPO Chris Cox, VP of AI Manohar Paluri, and researcher Angela Fan, followed by conversations between Mark Zuckerberg and Databricks CEO Ali Ghodsi, and later Satya Nadella.

## Key Announcements

### Meta AI App
- Standalone AI chatbot app announced (before conference began)
- Described as "assistant that gets to know your preferences, remembers context, is personalized to you"
- Includes Discover feed where users share how they use the assistant
- Replaces companion app for Meta AI glasses

### Llama API (Limited Preview)
- One-click API key creation
- Interactive playground to explore different Llama models
- Combines flexibility of open-source with developer convenience of closed APIs
- Direct competitor to OpenAI API developer experience

### Llama Protection Tools
- New suite of security evaluation tools
- **Llama Defenders Program:** Select trusted partners can access AI-enabled tools to evaluate security and safeguard against threats

### Llama Impact Grants (Round 2)
- 10 international recipients announced
- $1.5M+ USD in grants total
- Support for companies, startups, and universities using Llama for transformative change

## Community Impact
- Llama models used by Goldman Sachs, AT&T, DoorDash, NVIDIA, Snowflake
- Downloads passed 1 billion cumulative by March 2025; ~1.2 billion by late April 2025
- Used by researchers, practitioners, and developers worldwide

## Context

LlamaCon was organized to rebuild developer confidence after the mixed reception of Llama 4 (released April 5, 2025) amid benchmark controversy. The standalone Meta AI app was a direct response to ChatGPT's standalone app success.
