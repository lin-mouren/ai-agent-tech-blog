# Four MTIA Chips in Two Years: Scaling AI Experiences for Billions

March 11, 2026

The AI models that will power the next era of computing are evolving faster than ever. While we remain committed to a diverse silicon portfolio, the Meta Training and Inference Accelerator (MTIA), our family of homegrown AI chips developed in partnership with Broadcom, has been an important part of Meta's AI infrastructure strategy.

Since introducing MTIA 100 and 200, we have accelerated MTIA development across four successive generations: MTIA 300, 400, 450, and 500. These new chips have either already been deployed or are scheduled for deployment in 2026 or 2027, expanding workload coverage from ranking and recommendation (R&R) inference to R&R training, general GenAI workloads, and GenAI inference with targeted optimizations.

## Key advancements

Each generation of MTIA has built on the lessons of the one before, co-designed with our software stack, and guided by the trajectory of future AI models. Their modular, multi-chiplet design and vertically integrated co-design approach can deliver rapid, compounding performance gains while maintaining system-level compatibility.

The latest four generations - either recently launched or planned for 2026/2027 - push the boundaries of GenAI inference, enable R&R training, and lay the groundwork for future GenAI training.

Together, they bring us closer to our goal to deliver today and tomorrow's most powerful AI experiences to everyone on our platforms.
