# Bringing AI to the next generation of fusion energy

We're partnering with Commonwealth Fusion Systems (CFS) to bring clean, safe, limitless fusion energy closer to reality.

Fusion, the process that powers the sun, promises clean, abundant energy without long-lived radioactive waste. Making it work means keeping plasma stable at temperatures over 100 million degrees Celsius.

Today, we're announcing our research partnership with CFS, a global leader in fusion energy. CFS is pioneering a faster path to fusion energy with its compact tokamak machine called SPARC.

This partnership builds on our groundbreaking work using AI to successfully control a plasma. We showed that deep reinforcement learning can control the magnets of a tokamak to stabilize complex plasma shapes.

## Simulating fusion plasma

We developed TORAX, an open-source plasma simulator built for optimization and control. TORAX is built in JAX, so it can run on both CPUs and GPUs and integrate AI-powered models. It will help CFS teams test and refine operating plans.

## Finding the fastest path to maximum energy

Using TORAX with reinforcement learning or evolutionary search approaches like AlphaEvolve, our AI agents can explore vast numbers of potential operating scenarios in simulation, rapidly identifying the most efficient paths to generating net energy.

## Developing an AI pilot for real-time control

We're investigating how reinforcement learning agents can learn to dynamically control plasma to distribute heat effectively. The combination of pulse optimization and optimal control could push SPARC further and faster to achieve its historic goals.
