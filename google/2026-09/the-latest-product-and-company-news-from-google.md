### [Create your best tracks yet with Lyria 3.5 in Gemini.](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/products/gemini-app/better-tracks-lyria-gemini/)









[Lyria 3.5](https://deepmind.google/models/lyria/), our best-sounding music generation model, is now available in the [Gemini app](https://gemini.google.com/corp/app) and the [Gemini API](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/music-generation). Lyria 3.5 brings more expressive vocals and richer musical arrangements, allowing you to craft tracks with higher fidelity.



In the Gemini app, you can now:



  - Easily select or describe your genre and choose between vocal or instrumental styles
  - Use our new templates to jumpstart your creativity for anything from background music to custom birthday tracks
  - Plus, you’ll now have the flexibility to choose short or longer tracks

Whether you need a custom backing track for a video, a unique brand jingle, or just a personalized ringtone, it’s easier than ever to bring your idea to life.

Lyria 3.5 is available to all users globally on the [web](http://gemini.google/) and in the mobile app. It’s also available for artists and AI creatives in [Google Flow Music](https://www.flowmusic.app/), and for developers and technologists through [Google AI Studio](https://aistudio.google.com/new_music?model=lyria-3.5), and Google [Vids](https://workspace.google.com/products/vids/).

### [Google Translate rolls out new upgrades for iOS and Android.](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/translate/google-translate-ios-android-upgrades/)









Google Translate’s [live translate](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/translate/language-learning-live-translate/) brings natural, near-real time [audio translations](https://youtu.be/rPq7ITrWFvY) to 70+ languages. Today, we're introducing two updates to make translations even more seamless.



First, because more than a third of live translation sessions now last longer than five minutes, [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.google.android.apps.translate) users can now keep live translations running in the background. This is perfect for longer sessions where you need to multitask or want to lock your phone screen. iOS support is coming soon.



Second, [iOS](http://google.com/url?q=https://apps.apple.com/us/app/google-translate/id414706506&sa=D&source=docs&ust=1788282498703444&usg=AOvVaw0KEyYQmGUsZetjrtU5mtOY) users worldwide can now get live translations directly through their phone’s earpiece. (The feature is already available on Android.) When you don't have headphones handy, just hold your phone to your ear. It’s great for catching announcements at noisy airports or following along on crowded tours.



























Google Translate's latest updates: (Left) On Android, you can now keep live translations running while you're multitasking or with the screen locked. (Right) iOS users worldwide can now get live translations directly through their phone’s earpiece.
