# Protecting people from harmful manipulation

Helen King

March 26, 2026

As AI models get better at holding natural conversations, we must examine how these interactions affect people and society.

Building on a breadth of scientific research, today, we are releasing new findings on the potential for AI to be misused for harmful manipulation, specifically, its ability to alter human thought and behavior in negative and deceptive ways. With this latest study, we have created the first empirically validated toolkit to measure this kind of AI manipulation in the real world.

## Why harmful manipulation matters

Consider two scenarios: One AI model gives you facts to make a well-informed healthcare decision. Another AI model uses fear to pressure you to make an ill-informed decision. The first educates; the second harms.

Types of persuasion in human-AI interactions:

- **Beneficial (rational) persuasion:** Using facts and evidence to help people make choices aligned with their own interest
- **Harmful manipulation:** Exploiting emotional and cognitive vulnerabilities to trick people into making harmful choices

## Developing new evaluations

Testing involved nine studies with over 10,000 participants across the UK, the US, and India, focused on high-stakes areas such as finance (simulated investment scenarios) and health (dietary supplement preferences). AI was least effective at harmful manipulation on health-related topics.

Our findings show that success in one domain does not predict success in another, validating a targeted approach to testing for harmful manipulation in specific, high-stakes environments.

## Putting research into practice

We introduced an exploratory Harmful Manipulation Critical Capability Level (CCL) within our Frontier Safety Framework to track models with capabilities which could be misused to systematically change beliefs and behaviors.

These evaluations serve as the foundation for how we test our models, including Gemini 3 Pro, for harmful manipulation.

## Looking ahead

We are exploring how to ethically evaluate harmful manipulation in even higher-stakes situations - like discussions involving deeply held personal beliefs. Next, we will expand research to investigate how audio, video, and image inputs as well as agentic capabilities factor into AI manipulation.
