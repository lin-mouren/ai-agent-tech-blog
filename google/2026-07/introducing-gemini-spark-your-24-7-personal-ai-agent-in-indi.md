We all have a running list of daily digital tasks we wish someone else would just handle for us. If you’re a busy parent, imagine having someone sort through scattered school emails in your Gmail so you never miss another permission slip deadline.

Meet Gemini Spark: your 24/7 personal AI agent that can do exactly that. Officially expanding to Google AI Pro subscribers in India over the next few weeks, Spark works quietly in the background - even when your laptop is closed or your phone is locked. It operates on Google’s secure cloud infrastructure, which means it connects natively with your Google Workspace tools like Gmail, Docs, and Sheets with zero setup required.

Available to Google AI Ultra and Pro subscribers, Gemini Spark fundamentally shifts AI from a reactive assistant that answers your questions into an active partner that gets real work done for you, even while you’re sleeping. Driven by Gemini 3.6 Flash, this new tool is designed to handle your time-consuming digital chores under your direction, giving you time back to focus on what matters most.

Spark always operates under your direction. You choose whether to turn it on and what apps it connects to, and it is designed to ask you first before performing high-stakes actions like spending money or sending emails.

Here are some great prompts to try with Gemini Spark:

- _Free-spirited traveller_: “Whenever a new flight or hotel booking confirmation email arrives, automatically add the details to my ‘My Bali Trip Itinerary’ Google Sheet.” and “Find interesting local events and activities happening in Bali when I’m there and add them to my calendar on days I’ve got nothing else planned.”

- _Weekend maxxer_: “Every Friday at 8AM, find local events that you think I'd be interested in and add them to my rolling ‘Weekend plans’ Google Doc that I can check for inspiration on things to do. Include key info about the events or activities, including links to learn more. If there are 1-3 events that you highly recommend, go ahead and add them to my calendar.”

- _Pickleball fanatic:_ “Every Tuesday at 8am, log into and check the PickleMe app for free indoor evening courts. If only outdoor courts are available, alert me only if the forecast is clear.”

- _Wedding party maestro:_ “Find my wedding RSVPs in Gmail, organise the dietary restrictions into a table, and draft a summary email to our caterer.”

- _Savvy saver_: “Every month on the 1st, audit my digital invoices in Gmail from the last month. Notify me about any subscription price hikes, or trials that are about to end and pre-draft cancellation emails.”

- _Hustling entrepreneu_ r: “Scan my inbox for customer inquiries. Automatically draft an email response recommending my services and pricing packages based on their request, and save it to drafts for my review.”

- _Productive professional_: “Read through the last 50 emails that I wrote and turn it into a style guide for how I write emails. Turn that into a skill that gets used every time I ask you to draft emails for me. Call that skill ‘ghostwriter’.”

This is the next chapter for Google AI: agents that put the world’s information to work for you. So ask whatever’s on your mind, and Google can now do more for you than ever before.
