# We’re partnering with Screwfix to help the nation's tradespeople nail admin and grow their businesses using AI.

Jul 16, 2026

* * *

We’re teaming up with Screwfix, the UK's leading retailer to the trade, to help tradespeople across the country get more from the AI power tools already in their pockets.

Construction workers are leading the charge, with 86% using AI on the job. Yet, a massive guidance gap remains: 71% are eager to learn, but over half (54%) aren't sure who to ask. As a result, only 16% are extracting significant value from these tools.

To bridge this gap, we're bringing quick, practical tips directly to Screwfix stores nationwide. From Newcastle to Belfast, we’ll help the UK’s builders, plumbers, and sparkies [level up their AI skills](https://blog.google/company-news/inside-google/around-the-globe/google-europe/united-kingdom/googles-ai-works-for-britain-aims-to-help-stuck-brits-unlock-progression/) and unlock business growth.

We’ll share how AI can help hammer through admin, by turning handwritten notes into client proposals or rough sketches into 3D renderings. Because when small businesses thrive, the whole UK economy grows stronger.

Learn more about how to make AI work for your business at [grow.google](https://grow.google/intl/uk/).
