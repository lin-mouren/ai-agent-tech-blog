# Find out what’s new in the Gemini app in July's Gemini Drop.

Jul 31, 2026

* * *

Welcome to the July edition of [Gemini Drops](https://gemini.google/gemini-drops/). See what’s new in our latest Drop:

- Speak to Gemini on [macOS](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/products/gemini-app/speak-naturally-gemini-app-mac-os/). Create, edit, and summarize by speaking into any active window: Dictate clean text, transform highlighted content, or generate visuals right where your cursor is.
- [Gemini Spark](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/products/gemini-app/next-evolution-gemini-app/) is going global. Get things done 24/7, even after closing your laptop, now available worldwide.


[1](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/gemini/gemini-drop-july-2026/#footnote-1)
- [Gemini 3.6 Flash and Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/gemini-3-6-flash-3-5-flash-lite-3-5-flash-cyber/). Our latest Flash models are available now with better reasoning and greater speed.
- Add yourself to any image. Create content that looks like you by adding your avatar — no need to upload your image every time.
- Help that works across your apps: Dropbox, Zillow Rentals, and Viator. Link your favorite apps to Gemini and get more done with a single prompt.
- Generate images tailored to you. Use Gemini to create [deeply personalized images](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/products/gemini-app/personal-intelligence-nano-banana/) based on your unique interests and preferences. Now available to all users in the US.

Find out even more on the [Gemini Drops Hub](https://gemini.google/gemini-drops/).
