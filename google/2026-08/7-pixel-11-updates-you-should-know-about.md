The new [Pixel 11 lineup](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/devices/pixel/google-pixel-11-pro-xl/) is designed for Gemini Intelligence

[1](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/devices/pixel/pixel-11-features/#footnote-1)
and delivers impressive camera upgrades. From all-new image sensors to proactive and personalized assistance, our latest hardware is built to help you navigate your day-to-day so you can stay present for what matters most.

## 1\. Get timely support from Gemini Intelligence.

We’re introducing a bunch of updates that deliver timely, proactive help and help you get things done. On Pixel devices, Gemini can handle even more [multistep tasks](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/products/gemini-app/android-multi-step-tasks/) across your favorite apps, expanding to more than 40 apps and counting

[1](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/devices/pixel/pixel-11-features/#footnote-1)
along with plenty more features you’ll find first on Pixel.

That includes Rambler,

[2](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/devices/pixel/pixel-11-features/#footnote-2)
our Gemini-powered voice input feature, which is built for the way you actually talk. It delivers fast, accurate voice input that transcribes your point cleanly and concisely — even when you use filler words, like “um” and “ah.”

And Pixel 11 will offer timely, helpful information through easily glanceable cards in the apps you’re already using. So, for example, if a friend asks about a trip that’s coming up, a card with your booking details will pop up along with details if your flight is on time or delayed.

## 2\. Stay present with Magic Capture.

Life’s most precious moments can happen in a flash. And we don’t want you to miss living in the moment as you try to capture it. With Magic Capture, you’ll be able to automatically snap those blink-and-you’ll-miss-it baby smiles, playful pets, or hard-to-get action shots — like a basketball frozen in mid air — with a single tap, all while you stay present and engaged.

In a typical session, Magic Capture uses both on-device intelligence and Gemini models to analyze around 400 frames to deliver perfectly-timed, high-quality 12-megapixel photos. And Magic Capture can even automatically apply edits like crop and unblur, so your moments are ready to share. Even better, you’ll also get a video without needing to go back and forth between modes.

## 3\. Pick your aesthetic with Camera Looks.

Camera Looks allows you to choose the exact aesthetic you’re going for, right at the point of capture. Because while the Original Pixel look is widely loved, we know everyone has their own preferences.

## 5\. Take low-light pictures much faster.

Pixel has always been great at capturing the stars, and Instant Night Sight makes low-light photography better than ever on Pixel 11 Pro and Pixel 11 Pro XL. You’ll now be able to capture Night Sight images up to 4.5 times faster

[6](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/devices/pixel/pixel-11-features/#footnote-6)
while still retaining the quality you’ve come to expect. Because when you’re shooting in low light, you shouldn't have to choose between a great shot and holding still.



## 6\. Circle to search right from your camera.

Use Circle to Search in your Camera viewfinder to learn about things you see. For example, if you’re exploring a botanical garden, you can quickly point your camera at a flower, zoom in for a closer look, and use Circle to Search to instantly identify the species, find planting tips, or get more information.

## 7\. Translate real-world content without interruption.

Translate videos and audio in real time with Live Translate. This technology leverages Live Translate’s real-time speech-to-speech translation capability, advanced on-device generative AI models powered by our Google Tensor G6 processor, automatically dubbing media from around the world into your preferred language.
