# Here’s your first look at the newest Pixel devices.

Aug 12, 2026

* * *

Today, we unveiled [new Pixel devices](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/devices/pixel/made-by-google-2026) — including [new Pixel 11 phones](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/devices/pixel/google-pixel-11-pro-xl/), [Pixel Watch 5](http://blog.google/products-and-platforms/devices/pixel/pixel-watch-5), and our first-ever [Pixel Tag](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/devices/pixel/google-pixel-tag/). Here’s a look at everything in the lineup.

Our new phones — Pixel 11, Pixel 11 Pro, Pixel 11 Pro XL, and Pixel 11 Pro Fold — are designed for Gemini Intelligence.

[1](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/devices/pixel/new-pixel-devices-2026/#footnote-1)
They’re more durable and include [major camera upgrades](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/devices/pixel/pixel-11-features) that help you capture the moment in your authentic style.

Pixel Watch 5 delivers proactive Gemini Intelligence capabilities, personalized strength coaching, and our most accurate [GPS route tracking](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/devices/pixel/pixel-watch-5-gps). It also offers breakthrough Health Guardian features, like blood pressure and insulin resistance trends and [breathing emergency detection](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/devices/pixel/pixel-watch-breathing-emergency-detection) — an industry-first for a smartwatch.

Our first-ever finder tag, Pixel Tag, uses [Google’s Find Hub](https://www.google.com/android/find/) network to help you locate your favorite items. It’s made of durable stainless steel and has a long-lasting battery, and its built-in speaker plays a sound when prompted.

Visit the [Google Store](https://store.google.com/us/category/phones?utm_source=keyword&utm_medium=google_oo&utm_campaign=GS108864&utm_term=kw12&utm_content=blog) for more information on availability and product updates.
