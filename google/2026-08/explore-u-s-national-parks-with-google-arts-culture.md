While many people associate the national parks with sweeping natural beauty, they’re also some of the places where American history was written. To help safeguard these treasures for future generations — and make them accessible to people everywhere now — the National Park Service and Google Arts & Culture are launching [United Parks of America](https://goo.gle/unitedparksusa).

As we honor the [National Park Service’s 110th birthday](https://www.nps.gov/subjects/npscelebrates/nps-birthday.htm) and celebrate [National Park Week](https://www.nps.gov/subjects/npscelebrates/national-park-week.htm), we’re launching a new interactive hub bringing historic heritage and 60 of the nation’s protected natural wonders together under one digital roof. You can explore more than 150 curated exhibitions, 30 virtual tours, and over 500 miles of Street View imagery across iconic National Parks — from the depths of the Grand Canyon to the peaks of Yosemite.

Here are just a few ways to start your journey:

- Scale [majestic mountains](https://artsandculture.google.com/story/nwXxYl9zf3bDQg).
- Chase [spectacular waterfalls](https://artsandculture.google.com/story/jQXReqF4Mu-vDA) carved by nature.
- Go beneath the surface to explore [five hidden caves](https://artsandculture.google.com/story/hwVxz4rd6GsBBQ).
- Meet [John Muir](https://artsandculture.google.com/story/dgXRokL0OWMAxw), the man who taught the world how to truly see Yosemite.
- Learn about [Boston’s role](https://artsandculture.google.com/story/DwVxVh1XlypE9Q) in the American War of Independence, and discover [Independence National Historical Park](https://artsandculture.google.com/story/LQWBbQog3VXfsw), the birthplace of America.



View nature’s palette in full display with a virtual visit to the Grand Prismatic Spring at Yellowstone National Park.

## Step inside history with an AI experiment.

Explore history in a new way with [Path to Independence](https://goo.gle/path-to-independence), an experimental AI storytelling experience that takes place at Independence National Historical Park. Travel back 250 years to explore the Declaration of Independence through panoramic imagery and AI-narrated tours, diving deeper into the past with the help of Gemini.

- Explore the Assembly Room, the hall where the Founding Fathers signed the Declaration of Independence. Using Nano Banana technology, we’ve worked to help you imagine the 18th century as it might have been.
- Examine the Liberty Bell and primary source documents up close.
- Ask Gemini follow-up questions to receive instant, tailored historical insights and learn about the people and moments that forged a new nation through seamless audio narration.



Take a front-row seat inside Independence Hall with an interactive experiment that allows you to ask questions, test your knowledge, and inspect historic artifacts up close.

## Virtually visit America’s natural wonders and 3D artifacts.

Preserving our heritage means protecting the artifacts and art that tell our national story. We’ve digitized [fragile historical objects](https://artsandculture.google.com/story/JQUBsWpg6Y9u5g) from the Independence National Historical Park collection, including [19 copies of the Declaration of Independence](https://artsandculture.google.com/story/eQWBfekpnR-E1A), [149 18th century portraits](https://artsandculture.google.com/story/MQXRyZqKL2FuHw), and more than 2000 pages of historic documents. We’ve also developed interactive 3D models you can spin and zoom into. And you can celebrate the [National Park Service](https://www.nps.gov/subjects/npscelebrates/national-park-week.htm) by exploring unique landscapes across six beloved national parks.

- Discover the [ancient cliff dwellings](https://artsandculture.google.com/asset/3d-model-of-balcony-house-mesa-verde-national-park-usa/twEFhH7VrBwCPA) of the Ancestral Puebloans.
- See iconic [Mount Rushmore carvings](https://artsandculture.google.com/asset/mount-rushmore-national-memorial/PAHteMQ3yKwalw) from new perspectives.
- Explore the geological and biological history of America’s forests with [petrified tree trunks](https://artsandculture.google.com/asset/RgGnkC6BmwBn8Q).
- Experience the [Grand Canyon](https://artsandculture.google.com/entity/grand-canyon-national-park/m0cnrr)’s mile-deep gorges and [Acadia National Park](https://artsandculture.google.com/entity/acadia-national-park/m0gy14)’s rocky coastlines.
- Marvel at [Glacier National Park](https://artsandculture.google.com/entity/glacier-national-park/m01gznn)’s and [Yosemite](https://artsandculture.google.com/entity/yosemite-national-park/m0c_y8)’s iconic peaks.
- Experience unique ecosystems, like [Yellowstone](https://artsandculture.google.com/entity/yellowstone-national-park/m08874)’s explosive geothermal geysers and the diverse, ancient, mist-covered forests of the [Great Smoky Mountains](https://artsandculture.google.com/entity/great-smoky-mountains/m02z9hk).



From the rugged cliffs east of Mather Point to the glowing horizon, experience the breathtaking Grand Canyon National Park right from your screen on Google Arts & Culture.

The legacy of America’s parks and history is shared by us all. Visit the [United Parks of America](http://goo.gle/unitedparksusa) hub online to celebrate the heritage that connects us, or commemorate the National Park Service’s birthday by visiting a national park. August 25 is an entrance-fee-free day for U.S. citizens and residents.
