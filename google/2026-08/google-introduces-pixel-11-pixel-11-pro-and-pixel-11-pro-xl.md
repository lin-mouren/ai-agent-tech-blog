## All powered by Google Tensor G6

Powering some of our [most advanced camera experiences](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/devices/pixel/pixel-11-features/), Tensor G6 is our fastest, most powerful chip yet.

[13](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/devices/pixel/google-pixel-11-pro-xl/#footnote-13)

But we didn’t just build a faster chip; we built a purpose-driven engine designed to make your Pixel more intuitive, fluid, and helpful.

Tensor G6 features an upgraded CPU for 25% faster web browsing and 15% quicker app launches.

[14](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/devices/pixel/google-pixel-11-pro-xl/#footnote-14)
Packing 50% more TPU compute and paired with the latest Gemini Nano model, Google Tensor G6 processes on-device AI tasks up to 3.5 times faster while using up to 3.5 times less energy.

[15](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/devices/pixel/google-pixel-11-pro-xl/#footnote-15) [16](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/devices/pixel/google-pixel-11-pro-xl/#footnote-16)

As technology evolves, so do the threats. Google Pixel is engineered specifically to help safeguard your most sensitive information from advanced attacks today and from risks like attacks by quantum computers in the future. Our new Titan M3 security chip and custom Tensor G6 processor seamlessly integrate to form our most robust, proactive defense system yet, bringing post-quantum cryptography to secure boot on your device.

Everything on your Pixel works together — the software, the hardware, the processor, and the AI — to give you real-world protection. And according to a new report by [Counterpoint Research](https://counterpointresearch.com/en/reports/AI-privacy-and-security-scorecard-2026), our AI experience on Pixel has the most privacy protection on a phone.
