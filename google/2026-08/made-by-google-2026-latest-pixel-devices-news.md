When it comes to our devices, we're committed to building experiences that help you make the most of your time — all while helping you stay connected and present with the people who matter most.

That's the vision behind the Pixel portfolio of products. We’re simplifying those daily moments where you turn to your phone to get things done, ensuring our latest products are purpose-built to help you throughout the day.

By leveraging our full vertical tech stack, [our latest Pixel hardware](https://store.google.com/?utm_source=keyword&utm_medium=google_oo&utm_campaign=GS108864&utm_term=collection_page&utm_content=blog) is designed to help you reclaim the moment. Our approach is deeply personal, proactive, and productive, all with a single goal: to help you better navigate your life so you can get back to what you truly love.

Here’s everything we announced at Made by Google 2026.
