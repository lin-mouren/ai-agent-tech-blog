Kosovo is a captivating blend of modern energy and deep history. Starting today, you can virtually explore its unique charm, diverse landscapes, and cultural monuments through Google Street View. From the markets and cafes of Pristina to the rugged alpine peaks, this remarkable region is now just a click away.

Our Street View cars journeyed across Kosovo, capturing its evolving urban centers and timeless sights. Here are a few highlights of what you can now experience on Google Maps.

## Observe centuries of history.

Kosovo is home to breathtaking spiritual sites, many of which are recognized globally for their architectural and cultural significance. Take a virtual journey along the approaches to the [Gračanica Monastery](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Gra%C4%8Danica+Monastery/@42.5983664,21.1926524,3a,75y,85.63h,80.68t/data=!3m8!1e1!3m6!1sfuxHk7-EIwhYusJOBunVwQ!2e0!5s20250801T000000!6shttps:%2F%2Fstreetviewpixels-pa.googleapis.com%2Fv1%2Fthumbnail%3Fcb_client%3Dmaps_sv.tactile%26w%3D900%26h%3D600%26pitch%3D9.32367676295587%26panoid%3DfuxHk7-EIwhYusJOBunVwQ%26yaw%3D85.6337676325137!7i16384!8i8192!4m7!3m6!1s0x13549b89603b0297:0x63b853d64ac39927!8m2!3d42.5984875!4d21.1934844!10e5!16zL20vMDk3aF9u?deb=0svepoch1140&entry=ttu&g_ep=EgoyMDI2MDcyNi4wIKXMDSoASAFQAw%3D%3D), or navigate the roads leading to the entrance of the [Patriarchate of Peć Monastery](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Patriarchate+of+Pe%C4%87+(monastery)/@42.6611817,20.2696876,3a,75y,267.78h,103.56t/data=!3m8!1e1!3m6!1sdYnH5B8s9FdwSVoGV5aNEQ!2e0!5s20250901T000000!6shttps:%2F%2Fstreetviewpixels-pa.googleapis.com%2Fv1%2Fthumbnail%3Fcb_client%3Dmaps_sv.tactile%26w%3D900%26h%3D600%26pitch%3D-13.559930453341693%26panoid%3DdYnH5B8s9FdwSVoGV5aNEQ%26yaw%3D267.7805543069917!7i16384!8i8192!4m7!3m6!1s0x1352fc3bf9a9d553:0xde0f10ba1ff2cac6!8m2!3d42.6611011!4d20.2656512!10e5!16zL20vMDk3ajdf?deb=0svepoch1140&entry=ttu&g_ep=EgoyMDI2MDcyNi4wIKXMDSoASAFQAw%3D%3D), which are surrounded by striking mountain scenery. Head toward the [Visoki Dečani Monastery](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Monastery+Visoki+Decani/@42.5468734,20.2643816,3a,48.9y,88.64h,96.18t/data=!3m7!1e1!3m5!1sTIXTdQtKTM0MBFzk5kpCzg!2e0!6shttps:%2F%2Fstreetviewpixels-pa.googleapis.com%2Fv1%2Fthumbnail%3Fcb_client%3Dmaps_sv.tactile%26w%3D900%26h%3D600%26pitch%3D-6.180000000000007%26panoid%3DTIXTdQtKTM0MBFzk5kpCzg%26yaw%3D88.64!7i16384!8i8192!4m7!3m6!1s0x135255bbef3ab631:0x40b461713da03f2f!8m2!3d42.546279!4d20.2652581!10e5!16zL20vMDZ2eTI0?deb=0svepoch1140&entry=ttu&g_ep=EgoyMDI2MDcyNi4wIKXMDSoASAFQAw%3D%3D) to catch distant glimpses of the historic buildings, or explore the streets surrounding the 14th-century [Church of the Holy Virgin of Ljeviša](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Crkva+Bogorodica+Ljevi%C5%A1ka/@42.2116226,20.7358887,3a,90y,89.86h,103.43t/data=!3m7!1e1!3m5!1s_S7qq8HwYpNkAPYqjiBNKw!2e0!6shttps:%2F%2Fstreetviewpixels-pa.googleapis.com%2Fv1%2Fthumbnail%3Fcb_client%3Dmaps_sv.tactile%26w%3D900%26h%3D600%26pitch%3D-13.434061410373914%26panoid%3D_S7qq8HwYpNkAPYqjiBNKw%26yaw%3D89.85546480020895!7i8704!8i4352!4m7!3m6!1s0x1353950010f7d297:0xcd0b94a052501fb9!8m2!3d42.2116226!4d20.7358887!10e5!16s%2Fg%2F11yy_fzpbc?deb=0svepoch1140&entry=ttu&g_ep=EgoyMDI2MDcyNi4wIKXMDSoASAFQAw%3D%3D) in Prizren. These landmarks make up the Medieval Monuments in Kosovo UNESCO World Heritage site, serving as a testament to the region's rich history.

Continue your travels in Prizren, a city widely considered Kosovo's cultural capital. Visit the 17th-century [Gazi Mehmed Pasha Hammam](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Gazi+Mehmed+Pasha+Hammam+(Turkish+Bath)/@42.2106401,20.7414064,3a,75y,15.11h,92.38t/data=!3m8!1e1!3m6!1suD2lTkIYmkRicU1afU0i-A!2e0!5s20250901T000000!6shttps:%2F%2Fstreetviewpixels-pa.googleapis.com%2Fv1%2Fthumbnail%3Fcb_client%3Dmaps_sv.tactile%26w%3D900%26h%3D600%26pitch%3D-2.3797731901961896%26panoid%3DuD2lTkIYmkRicU1afU0i-A%26yaw%3D15.10766151341372!7i16384!8i8192!4m7!3m6!1s0x1353950ddbb7cb13:0x774172bc0287842c!8m2!3d42.2107791!4d20.741583!10e5!16s%2Fg%2F1tq134ry?deb=0svepoch1140&entry=ttu&g_ep=EgoyMDI2MDcyNi4wIKXMDSoASAFQAw%3D%3D) and admire the [Sinan Pasha Mosque](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Sinan+Pasha+Mosque/@42.20976,20.7415787,3a,75y,208.12h,96.94t/data=!3m8!1e1!3m6!1sps-E7MSyngdRgXmXhZooVQ!2e0!5s20250901T000000!6shttps:%2F%2Fstreetviewpixels-pa.googleapis.com%2Fv1%2Fthumbnail%3Fcb_client%3Dmaps_sv.tactile%26w%3D900%26h%3D600%26pitch%3D-6.939999999999998%26panoid%3Dps-E7MSyngdRgXmXhZooVQ%26yaw%3D208.12!7i16384!8i8192!4m7!3m6!1s0x1353951205c1648b:0x808b3eeaaf716e31!8m2!3d42.2089806!4d20.7413731!10e5!16s%2Fm%2F0cp1fb3?deb=0svepoch1140&entry=ttu&g_ep=EgoyMDI2MDcyNi4wIKXMDSoASAFQAw%3D%3D), which dominates the town's majestic skyline. Stroll down to the river to see the [Old Stone Bridge](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Old+Stone+Bridge/@42.2097613,20.7406162,3a,75y,187.36h,96.64t/data=!3m8!1e1!3m6!1svLbNM6BcDq6jb9H_CZh8QA!2e0!5s20250901T000000!6shttps:%2F%2Fstreetviewpixels-pa.googleapis.com%2Fv1%2Fthumbnail%3Fcb_client%3Dmaps_sv.tactile%26w%3D900%26h%3D600%26pitch%3D-6.640000000000001%26panoid%3DvLbNM6BcDq6jb9H_CZh8QA%26yaw%3D187.36!7i16384!8i8192!4m7!3m6!1s0x135395441502bf4f:0x3c6a7b7aada8c20c!8m2!3d42.2094881!4d20.7406208!10e5!16s%2Fg%2F121thx78?deb=0svepoch1140&expflags=__data_rollout__Tactile.IsDogfoodRollout__launched__:false,__data_rollout__Tactile.IsFishfoodRollout__launched__:false&entry=ttu&g_ep=EgoyMDI2MDcyNi4wIKXMDSoASAFQAw%3D%3D).

In Gjakova, immerse yourself in history by visiting the [Tanner’s Bridge](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Tanners'+bridge/@42.3704941,20.4219434,3a,41.2y,273.4h,89.25t/data=!3m8!1e1!3m6!1sZR181wRx13jaVCsKdOIkcA!2e0!5s20251001T000000!6shttps:%2F%2Fstreetviewpixels-pa.googleapis.com%2Fv1%2Fthumbnail%3Fcb_client%3Dmaps_sv.tactile%26w%3D900%26h%3D600%26pitch%3D0.7537132297383238%26panoid%3DZR181wRx13jaVCsKdOIkcA%26yaw%3D273.40438032828985!7i16384!8i8192!4m7!3m6!1s0x1353b35e63565f4d:0xfd7a2d9d8e16f367!8m2!3d42.3705002!4d20.4208505!10e5!16s%2Fg%2F11lt7hj046?deb=0svepoch1140&entry=ttu&g_ep=EgoyMDI2MDcyNi4wIKXMDSoASAFQAw%3D%3D) or the impressive [Terzi Bridge](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Ura+e+Terzive+-+Tailors+bridge/@42.3594027,20.5095688,3a,75y,163.58h,85.39t/data=!3m8!1e1!3m6!1sQvzCFop4Fn7QV3o6LdyeeA!2e0!5s20251001T000000!6shttps:%2F%2Fstreetviewpixels-pa.googleapis.com%2Fv1%2Fthumbnail%3Fcb_client%3Dmaps_sv.tactile%26w%3D900%26h%3D600%26pitch%3D4.611123976856916%26panoid%3DQvzCFop4Fn7QV3o6LdyeeA%26yaw%3D163.58300076759562!7i16384!8i8192!4m7!3m6!1s0x1353b16f58f727e1:0x31784eec498a4c3e!8m2!3d42.3591556!4d20.5096775!10e5!16s%2Fm%2F09gqfjd?deb=0svepoch1140&entry=ttu&g_ep=EgoyMDI2MDcyNi4wIKXMDSoASAFQAw%3D%3D), a masterpiece of Ottoman architecture built over the Erenik River.
