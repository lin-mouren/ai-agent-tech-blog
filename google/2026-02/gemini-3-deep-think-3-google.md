# Gemini 3 Deep Think: Advancing science, research and engineering

Today, we're releasing a major upgrade to Gemini 3 Deep Think, our specialized reasoning mode, built to push the frontier of intelligence and solve modern challenges across science, research, and engineering.

We updated Gemini 3 Deep Think in close partnership with scientists and researchers to tackle tough research challenges where problems often lack clear guardrails or a single correct solution.

The new Deep Think is now available in the Gemini app for Google AI Ultra subscribers and, for the first time, via the Gemini API to select researchers, engineers and enterprises.

## Elevating reasoning with mathematical and algorithmic rigor

The updated Deep Think mode continues to push the frontiers of intelligence:
- Setting a new standard (48.4%, without tools) on Humanity's Last Exam
- Achieving an unprecedented 84.6% on ARC-AGI-2, verified by the ARC Prize Foundation
- Attaining a staggering Elo of 3455 on Codeforces
- Reaching gold-medal level performance on the International Math Olympiad 2025

## Navigating complex scientific domains

Beyond mathematics, Deep Think excels across chemistry and physics, demonstrating gold medal-level results on the 2025 International Physics Olympiad and Chemistry Olympiad.

## Accelerating real-world engineering

Deep Think is built to drive practical applications, enabling researchers to interpret complex data and engineers to model physical systems through code. With the updated Deep Think, you can turn a sketch into a 3D-printable reality.
