If you’ve been dreaming of your next vacanza, we’ve got just the thing to help you live out your 🎵"Future Nostalgia."🎵

Starting today, Google Maps is teaming up with global editorial and cultural platform Service95 and its founder, singer-songwriter Dua Lipa. As the world’s vacanza queen, Dua is sharing her personal travel guide, right in Maps — and she's bringing a custom Pegman along for the ride.

## Get recommendations from Dua

Dua and Service95 just dropped 12 shared lists in Maps that are packed with her favorite spots around the world, and each includes a pic from her camera roll and her personal recommendations. Whether you’re looking for late-night tacos in Mexico City or a romantic spot in Paris for a candlelit date, these lists give you a front-row seat to Dua’s travels. Check out her lists below — or find them in Maps when you search for dozens of cities, including London, Los Angeles, Tokyo or Barcelona. You build your own Dua-inspired trip by saving places you want to visit to your own map.
