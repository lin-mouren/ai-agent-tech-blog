# Deepening our partnership with the UK AI Security Institute

**December 11, 2025** | Google DeepMind (William Isaac and Owen Larter)

## Overview

Google DeepMind announced an expanded partnership with the UK AI Security Institute (AISI) through a new Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) focused on foundational security and safety research.

This builds on a partnership with AISI since its inception in November 2023, during which Google DeepMind tested its most capable models.

## What the New Partnership Involves

- Sharing access to proprietary models, data, and ideas to accelerate research
- Joint reports and publications sharing findings with the research community
- More collaborative security and safety research combining teams' expertise
- Technical discussions to tackle complex safety challenges
- Priority technical access to frontier models provided to AISI

## Key Research Areas

### 1. Monitoring AI Reasoning Processes (Chain-of-Thought)

Developing techniques to monitor an AI system's "thinking" (chain-of-thought / CoT). Builds on:
- Previous Google DeepMind research
- Recent collaboration with AISI, OpenAI, Anthropic, and other partners on Chain of Thought Monitorability

### 2. Understanding Social and Emotional Impacts

Investigating ethical implications of **socioaffective misalignment**: the potential for AI models to behave in ways that don't align with human wellbeing, even when technically following instructions correctly.

### 3. Evaluating Economic Systems

Exploring the potential impact of AI on economic systems by simulating real-world tasks across different environments. Focus on:
- Long-term labour market impact
- Task complexity and representativeness
- AI's role in economic productivity changes

## Broader Safety Strategy

Google DeepMind's overall AI safety approach includes:
- Foresight research
- Extensive safety training alongside capability development
- Rigorous testing and external partnerships with Apollo Research, Vaultis, Dreadnode, and others
- Responsibility and Safety Council for internal governance
- Membership in Frontier Model Forum and Partnership on AI
