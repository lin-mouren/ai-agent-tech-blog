# Introducing Gemini 3 Flash: frontier intelligence built for speed

**December 17, 2025** | Google (Tulsee Doshi, on behalf of the Gemini team)

## Overview

Google released Gemini 3 Flash, expanding the Gemini 3 model family with a model that combines frontier intelligence with Flash-level latency, efficiency, and cost.

Gemini 3 Flash is now the **default model in the Gemini app** (replacing 2.5 Flash) and the default for AI Mode in Search.

## Key Performance

Gemini 3 Flash delivers frontier performance:
- **GPQA Diamond**: 90.4% (PhD-level reasoning)
- **Humanity's Last Exam**: 33.7% without tools
- **MMMU Pro**: 81.2% (comparable to Gemini 3 Pro)
- **SWE-bench Verified**: 78% (coding agent capabilities)
- **Speed**: 3x faster than 2.5 Pro (Artificial Analysis benchmarking)
- **Cost**: Uses 30% fewer tokens on average than 2.5 Pro

## Pricing

- Input: $0.50/1M tokens
- Output: $3/1M tokens
- Audio input: $1/1M input tokens

## Key Features

### Pro-Grade Reasoning at Flash Speed
- Combines Gemini 3 Pro's reasoning capabilities with Flash-level latency
- Ideal for iterative development and high-frequency workflows
- Excels at agentic coding, production-ready systems, and responsive interactive applications

### Multimodal Capabilities
- Advanced visual and spatial reasoning
- Video analysis (turns video content into actionable plans)
- Real-time drawing interpretation (guesses what you're drawing as you sketch)
- Audio recording analysis (identifies knowledge gaps, creates custom quizzes)
- App building from voice instructions without prior coding knowledge

### Agentic Performance
- Most impressive Gemini model for agentic workflows
- Capable of complex video analysis, data extraction, and visual Q&A
- Strong tool use for multi-step task coordination

## Availability

Gemini 3 Flash is available across:
- **Gemini app** (global default, replacing 2.5 Flash)
- **AI Mode in Search** (global default)
- **Google AI Studio** (preview via Gemini API)
- **Google Antigravity** (new agentic development platform)
- **Gemini CLI** and **Android Studio**
- **Vertex AI** and **Gemini Enterprise**

## Business Adoption

Companies including JetBrains, Bridgewater Associates, and Figma are already using Gemini 3 Flash, recognizing how its inference speed, efficiency, and reasoning capabilities perform on par with larger models.
