# Google DeepMind & DOE Partner on Genesis: AI for Science

**December 18, 2025** | Google DeepMind (Pushmeet Kohli and Tom Lue)

## Overview

Google DeepMind is supporting the White House's Genesis Mission—a historic national effort to use AI to transform how scientific research is conducted and accelerate the speed of American science. The Genesis Mission will mobilize the U.S. Department of Energy's (DOE) 17 National Laboratories, industry, and academia to build an integrated discovery platform.

## The Partnership

Google and the DOE are partnering to support the Administration's goal of:
- Dramatically expanding the productivity and impact of American research and innovation within a decade
- Harnessing the AI and advanced computing revolution
- Accelerating breakthroughs across energy, scientific discovery, and national security

## AI Tools for National Laboratories

### Immediate Access: AI Co-Scientist
Google DeepMind provides an accelerated access program for scientists at all 17 DOE National Laboratories, starting with **AI co-scientist** on Google Cloud:
- A multi-agent virtual scientific collaborator built on Gemini
- Trained on Google's world-class TPUs
- Designed to synthesize vast information and generate novel hypotheses
- Has already proposed validated drug repurposing candidates for liver fibrosis
- Predicted complex antimicrobial resistance mechanisms that matched experiments before publication

### Early 2026: Additional Tools
- **AlphaEvolve** — Gemini-powered coding agent for designing advanced algorithms
- **AlphaGenome** — AI model to help scientists understand non-coding DNA
- **WeatherNext** — state-of-the-art family of weather forecasting models

## Historical Context

The AlphaFold development benefited from foundational work by DOE's Brookhaven National Laboratory on the Protein Data Bank. The AlphaFold Protein Database has been used by more than **3 million scientists in over 190 countries**, leading to Demis Hassabis and John Jumper co-receiving the 2024 Nobel Prize in Chemistry.

## Future Research Areas

Through the Genesis Mission, Google DeepMind will explore research collaborations in:
- Fusion energy
- New materials discovery
- Earth science

Additionally, National Labs can access **Gemini for Government**, Google's AI-optimized and accredited commercial cloud platform with frontier Gemini models.
