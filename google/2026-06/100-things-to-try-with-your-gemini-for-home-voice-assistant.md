## **Morning: Start your day right**

### Wake up the home   [1](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/devices/google-nest/gemini-home-voice-assistant-tips/\#footnote-1)

1. Control only the lights you want to: “Hey Google, turn on all the lights, except for the bedrooms."
2. Easily correct a command mid-sentence if you change your mind: “Hey Google, turn off the coffee maker, actually, I mean on."
3. Get the temperature just right: “Hey Google, it’s a little chilly this morning, can you heat things up?”
4. Check the camera history for what happened last night: “Hey Google, I heard a noise in my backyard last night. Was it an animal?”


[2](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/devices/google-nest/gemini-home-voice-assistant-tips/#footnote-2)



### Get fueled for the day

5\. Reach your wellness goals: “Hey Google, suggest a smoothie recipe that's high in fiber and will keep me going until lunchtime.”

6\. Start a new breakfast routine: “Hey Google, add all the ingredients to make strawberry overnight oats to my shopping list.”

7\. Hone your culinary techniques: “Hey Google, how do I cook my eggs over-easy without breaking the yolk?” or “How can I prevent the avocado in my sandwich from going brown by lunchtime?”

8\. Broadcast announcements to keep the home running on schedule: “Hey Google, let everyone know it’s time for breakfast.”

### Catch up on world events

9\. Get a conversational overview of today's headlines: “Hey Google, what's the news today?” Follow up: “Tell me more about the second story.”

10\. Discover what's happening in your local area: “Hey Google, what's the local news this morning?"

11\. Explore topics that interest you: “Hey Google, tell me the latest news about the space station mission.” Follow up: “What are the main scientific goals?”

12\. Understand how the news affects you: “Hey Google, can you explain what inflation actually means for my wallet?”

### Get ready for the day

13\. Prepare for the day: “Hey Google, do I need a jacket today?”

14\. Beat the morning traffic: “Hey Google, how is the traffic to work right now?”

15\. Get a summary of your plans for the week: “What’s on my calendar for the week?”

## **Mid-day: Multi-tasking**

### Knock-out multiple chores at once

16\. Do even more with one command: “Hey Google, set a 20-minute timer for the chicken, start the vacuum cleaner, and play some relaxing music."

[1](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/devices/google-nest/gemini-home-voice-assistant-tips/#footnote-1)

17\. Conquer your to-do list: "Hey Google, remind me to clean out the freezer and take out the trash when I get home from work today."

18\. Lock up and tidy the house as you walk out the door: “Hey Google, turn off all the lights, close the garage door, and start the robot vacuum."

[1](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/devices/google-nest/gemini-home-voice-assistant-tips/#footnote-1)

### Manage your to-dos

19\. Recall the lists you’ve made in Google Keep: “Hey Google, what lists do I have?”

20\. Get all the recipe ingredients added to your grocery list just by describing it: “Hey Google, I want to make chicken piccata – can you add the ingredients to my grocery list?”

21\. Quickly remove a category of items from your list: “OK Google, remove all vegetables from my shopping list.”

22\. Manage a category of items in bulk: “OK Google, move all the gardening supplies from my shopping list to a new gardening list.”

23\. Add to an existing note: “Hey Google, add 'Meeting went well' to my Journal note.”



### Master your calendar

24\. Get a summary of upcoming events: “Hey Google, what summer camps does Olivia have scheduled in July?”

25\. Move an event on your calendar: “OK Google, move back volleyball practice by an hour.”

26\. We’ve improved how Gemini handles recurring and all-day events. And, you can now simply ask about your upcoming events or move them around: “OK Google, set a reminder to water the plants every two weeks.”

27\. Check your availability: “Hey Google, which Saturday afternoons are free this month?” Follow up with “Can you add ‘play date with Scarlett’ to the next free Saturday afternoon?”

### Check-in on what’s happening around the house    [2](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/devices/google-nest/gemini-home-voice-assistant-tips/\#footnote-2)

28\. Pinpoint exactly when someone left without scrolling through video footage: “Hey Google, when did the white Honda leave the driveway?"

29\. Check in on after-school routines: “Hey Google, did the kids play in the backyard this afternoon?”

30\. Confirm security: “Hey Google, did the garage door close after the delivery truck left?”

31\. See what needs to be done: “Hey Google, are the garbage cans still out front?”

32\. Confirm if help has arrived. Once you’ve tagged a familiar face, Gemini automatically checks your doorbell history to answer your questions directly: “Hey Google, when did the nanny arrive yesterday?”

33\. Know the kids got home safely: “Hey Google, what time did the kids get home from school?”

### Stay focused and productive while working from home

34\. Learn time-management strategies: “Hey Google, explain the Pomodoro Technique to me.”

35\. Get tips for a midday work break: “OK Google, give me some quick ways to relieve eye strain.”

36\. Organize your tasks: “Hey Google, what are the best ways to organize my daily to-do list?”

37\. Cut down on distractions: “Hey Google, how can I reduce screen time and stay focused while working from home?”

38\. Incorporate mindfulness into your day: “OK Google, what are some simple breathing exercises to reduce stress?” Follow up: “Set a daily reminder to do this at 12 p.m.”

### Put on your favorite background music

39\. Find some music to support your flow: “Hey Google, what’s the best kind of music to listen to while studying?”

40\. Can’t remember your playlist’s exact name? Just ask: “Hey Google, play my jams playlist.”

41\. Hear new music from your favorite artists: “OK Google, play the latest album by \[insert artist name\].”

42\. Stream the top tracks from an artist: “Hey Google, play the best songs by \[insert artist name\].”

43\. Look up the story behind the lyrics: “OK Google, what do the lyrics from this song mean?”



## Evening: The dinner rush

### Get inspiration for dinner

44\. Whip up a fast weeknight meal: “Hey Google, what's a good 20-minute recipe that uses ground turkey?”

45\. Find cozy, seasonal comfort food: “Hey Google, give me an idea for a refreshing summer soup.”

46\. Cook with what's already in your pantry: “Hey Google, what can I make for dinner using a can of black beans, and dry pasta?”

47\. Reach your health goals: “Hey Google, teach me a low-sodium marinade for grilled chicken.”

48\. Turn something simple into a dinner the whole family will enjoy: “Hey Google, I’m just making ham sandwiches for dinner, but what can I do to make it special and delicious so my kids will eat it?”

### Prep in the kitchen

49\. Improvise while you’re cooking: “Hey Google, I don't have pine nuts, what else can I use for pesto?”

50\. Take on new culinary challenges: “Hey Google, what’s the best way to prepare a 3-pound tomahawk steak? I like it medium-rare.”

51\. Follow up on active timers naturally: “OK Google, how much time is left on my timer?” “How about now?”

52\. Control multiple timers at once: “Hey Google, pause all my baking timers.”

53\. Keep your preparation on schedule by saying: “Hey Google, preheat the smart oven to 350°."

[1](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/devices/google-nest/gemini-home-voice-assistant-tips/#footnote-1)

### Set the ambiance   [1](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/devices/google-nest/gemini-home-voice-assistant-tips/\#footnote-1)

54\. Light up your rooms by describing the look you want: “Hey Google, set the living room lights to the color of the ocean.”

55\. Control your smart home more naturally: “Hey Google, turn off the dining room lights, actually, just dim them.”

### Make family routines easier

56\. Assign chores to family members: “Hey Google, add ‘feed the dog’ to Leo’s chore list.”

57\. Keep track of what still needs to get done: “OK Google, what’s left to do on the family chore list today?”

58\. Stay on top of time-sensitive school paperwork: “Hey Google, remind me to sign the permission slips by Friday.”

59\. Deliver a message to a specific room: “Hey Google, broadcast to the kitchen, when do we leave for dinner?”

60\. Choose which rooms don’t hear the announcement: “Hey Google, broadcast it’s dinner time except for the baby room.”

### Help with homework

61\. Tackle tough questions from curious kids: “Hey Google, how do I explain black holes to a kindergartener?”

62\. Look up quick facts: “Hey Google, why is the ocean salty?”

63\. Explore nature’s wonders: “Hey Google, how does a caterpillar turn into a butterfly?”

64\. Settle a debate: “Hey Google, my friend said time moves slower if you travel faster. Is that true?”

65\. Demystify complex science: “Hey Google, how exactly does gravity bend light?”

66\. Master tricky grammar rules: “Hey Google, how do I know when to use 'who' versus 'whom'?”

## **Nighttime: Wind down the day**

### Create a relaxing environment

67\. Get more accurate controls for your entire room: “Hey Google, turn off the kitchen."

[1](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/devices/google-nest/gemini-home-voice-assistant-tips/#footnote-1)

68\. Make follow-up adjustments to get your home just right: “Hey Google, set the thermostat to 72 degrees." Follow up: “Make it a little warmer."

[1](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/devices/google-nest/gemini-home-voice-assistant-tips/#footnote-1)

69\. Fall asleep without playing media all night: “OK Google, set a sleep timer for 45 minutes.”

### Decompress and rest

70\. Get a solid documentary recommendation: “Hey Google, suggest a highly-rated documentary about nature.”

71\. Look for a quick TV show to stream: “Hey Google, what's a good comedy series with 30-minute episodes?”

72\. Look up recent sci-fi favorites: “Hey Google, what are the top-rated sci-fi books released in the last five years?”

73\. Get recommendations on good biographies: “Hey Google, suggest biographies of interesting but mostly unknown women in history.”



### Find peace of mind   [2](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/devices/google-nest/gemini-home-voice-assistant-tips/\#footnote-2)

74\. Keep an eye on live movement on your property: “Hey Google, is anyone in the backyard right now?”

75\. Close up the home before bed: “Hey Google, make sure all the doors are locked and the garage door is closed.”

76\. Double-check your home's security: “Hey Google, did the lawn care team close the side gate when they left?”

77\. Catch up on your household while you were out: “Hey Google, what happened in my home today?”

### Prepare for tomorrow

78\. Ask about your day ahead: “Hey Google, what’s my schedule for tomorrow?”

79\. Make sure you don’t oversleep: “Hey Google, I have an early meeting tomorrow and need to wake up 30 minutes earlier."

80\. Plan your outfit or commute with a quick weather check: “Hey Google, will I need an umbrella tomorrow?”

### Say goodnight

81\. Ask for a bedtime story: “Hey Google, tell me a bedtime story about a dragon who is afraid of fire.” Follow up: “Add a friendly knight who teaches him how to roast marshmallows.”

82\. Do even more within one seamless command: “Hey Google, shut off all the lights, set the thermostat to 68 degrees and turn off the living room TV."

[1](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/devices/google-nest/gemini-home-voice-assistant-tips/#footnote-1)

83\. Find energy savings by switching your thermostat’s settings: “OK Google, set the thermostat to Eco mode."

[1](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/devices/google-nest/gemini-home-voice-assistant-tips/#footnote-1)

## Weekend: Upgrade your activities



### Plan a family outing

84\. Brainstorm weekend plans: “Hey Google, let’s chat about what to do this weekend."

[3](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/devices/google-nest/gemini-home-voice-assistant-tips/#footnote-3)

85\. Pick the perfect day for your outdoor plans: “Hey Google, is Saturday or Sunday better for a hike in Muir Woods?”

86\. Make sure you’re prepared: “Hey Google, what’s a delicious healthy trail mix I can make for my hike?” Follow up with: “Make a note of that, and add the ingredients to my shopping list.”

87\. See how the conditions look before you make plans: “Hey Google, what's the surf forecast at Ocean Beach for tomorrow?”

88\. Make sure it’s nice out: “Hey Google, will it be sunny enough for a picnic in Dolores Park on Sunday?”

89\. Plan your watch parties for major events: “Hey Google, when is the next lunar eclipse?” Follow up with: “Put it on my calendar, and remind me the week before so I can make plans.”

90\. Find activities close to home: “Hey Google, what farmers' markets are happening around town this Sunday?”

91\. Make the most of your trip to the market: “Hey Google, move all of my fresh produce from the grocery list to the ‘farmer’s market’ list.”

### Pick up a new hobby

92\. Get started on learning your favorite instrument: “Hey Google, what are the basic chords I need to learn to play the acoustic guitar?”

93\. Find out how to take better photos: “Hey Google, how can I improve my photography skills using just my smartphone?”

94\. Pick an easy first project: “Hey Google, let’s chat. What's a good beginner project for someone learning how to knit?"

[3](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/devices/google-nest/gemini-home-voice-assistant-tips/#footnote-3)

95\. Find beginner-friendly classics: “Hey Google, give me a list of classic literature that's actually an easy read.”

96\. Manage your niche hobby gear: “Hey Google, turn on the monstera grow light.” Follow up with: “Dim the lights a little bit."

[1](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/devices/google-nest/gemini-home-voice-assistant-tips/#footnote-1)

### Host a gathering

97\. Get the right amount of food: “Hey Google, how many pounds of tri-tip do I need for a BBQ with six people?”

98\. Get your recipe quantities exactly right: “Hey Google, how many avocados should I buy to make a large bowl of guacamole?”

99\. Design the perfect spread: “Hey Google, plan a vegetarian brunch menu for six people featuring seasonal spring produce.”

100\. Keep your preparations on schedule: “Hey Google, guests are arriving at 6:30 p.m., and I want to serve dinner at 7:15 p.m. When do I need to put the chuck roast in the oven?”

For even more ideas, check out our previous list of [100 things to try with the Gemini for Home voice assistant](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/devices/google-nest/gemini-for-home-things-to-try/).
