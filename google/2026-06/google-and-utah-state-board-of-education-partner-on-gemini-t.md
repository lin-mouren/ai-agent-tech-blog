Today, we’re announcing a new partnership with the [Utah State Board of Education](https://www.schools.utah.gov/) (USBE) to provide access to [Gemini for Education](https://edu.google.com/intl/ALL_us/ai/gemini-for-education/) for every K-12 school across Utah. Starting in the 2026-2027 school year, this partnership will provide secure AI tools, training and [Google Career Certificates](https://www.skills.google/collections/career-certificates) to over 708,000 students and educators at no cost.

Utah is home to a vibrant academic community, encompassing roughly 680,000 K-12 learners. Supporting this next generation are more than 28,000 K-12 teachers across the state. With Google for Education’s AI tools, we want to help educators with personalized instruction and provide students with the resources they need to excel.

## Helping educators and students save time and expand learning

For educators, Gemini is designed to help streamline administrative tasks and enhance lesson planning. Educators can use these tools to generate customized materials like class assignments, grading rubrics, and summarize classroom discussions, which can help save time to allow educators more time for focused, direct student mentorship. As part of the rollout, USBE and Google will host a statewide webinar on the training opportunities to help ensure seamless implementation across the state.

For students, the technology can help them explore complex concepts, generate creative ideas for projects and receive additional explanations tailored to their needs. Our customized learning tools, like [Guided Learning](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/education/guided-learning/), takes you step-by-step to help you build a deep understanding instead of just getting quick answers. We worked with educators to design Guided Learning to be a partner in their teaching, built on the core principle that real learning is an active, constructive process.

## Protecting data and privacy

Privacy is at the core of our education tools. Your content and conversations within Gemini for Education are private, protected with enterprise-grade security, and are not used to train our AI models. Schools maintain full administrative control, helping to ensure a safe digital environment for everyone.

"We want our schools to be places where curiosity meets capability. By providing the option of integrating Gemini into Utah’s classrooms safely and responsibly, we are giving both our educators and our 680,000 students the tools they need to expand their learning, explore new possibilities and develop lifelong critical thinking skills.”

S

Superintendent Hart

Utah State Board of Education

## Providing free training and career skills

In addition to the tools, we’re providing free, specialized training on AI literacy and digital citizenship.

- The [Google AI Educator Series](https://edu.google.com/intl/ALL_us/learning-center/google-ai-educator-series/) offers practical lessons on using AI to improve student learning.
- [Google Career Certificates and AI training](https://grow.google/ai) are available at no cost for students, faculty, and staff (Utah grades 9-12) through December 2027, helping them gain skills for high-demand jobs in fields like cybersecurity and data analytics. These industry-recognized credentials are recommended by the [American Council on Education](https://www.acenet.edu/) (ACE) for up to 16 credits.

Together with the USBE, we’re excited to help Utah’s students prepare for the future. Find out more in [USBE’s press release](https://www.schools.utah.gov/publicrelations/_publicrelations_/_2026/USBEAdoptsGeminiEducationPressRelease.pdf).
