# Google Fitbit Air is now HSA/FSA eligible in the U.S.

The Google Fitbit Air

[1](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/devices/fitbit/buy-fitbit-air-pre-tax-health-savings/#footnote-1)
is now officially [certified as Health Savings Account (HSA) and Flexible Spending Account (FSA) eligible for qualifying customers](https://support.google.com/googlehealth/answer/17037428), giving you more ways to prioritize your wellbeing without the extra financial friction. Whether you’re looking to invest in your long-term health or simply want to optimize your daily routine, you may be eligible now to use your pre-tax health savings funds to purchase Fitbit Air, starting today.

[2](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/devices/fitbit/buy-fitbit-air-pre-tax-health-savings/#footnote-2) [3](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/devices/fitbit/buy-fitbit-air-pre-tax-health-savings/#footnote-3)

With the addition of HSA/FSA flexibility, our goal is to make advanced wellness accessible, affordable and seamless for everyone. Head over to the [Google Store](https://store.google.com/us/product/google_fitbit_air?hl=en-US&srsltid=AfmBOorrdRYMzUdixQH4LW5-J2fV6CIhGY3hVWRErEm8AgNfxB6gs-go) today to shop Fitbit Air and put your pre-tax healthcare dollars to work.
