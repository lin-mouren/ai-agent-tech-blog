New Zealand startups can now apply for the [Google for Startups Accelerator](https://startup.google.com/programs/accelerator/australia-new-zealand/). For the first time, we are expanding our program across the Tasman to back Kiwi innovation.

From world-class creative tech to human-centric software, Aotearoa New Zealand has always punched above its weight on the global stage. Now, as artificial intelligence (AI) opens a massive new frontier for productivity and creative problem-solving, Kiwi startups are uniquely primed to build what comes next.

Aimed at supercharging that talent, the Google for Startups Accelerator is an intensive 10-week, equity-free accelerator. Running from September to November as a hybrid program, it’s tailored specifically for Seed and Series A startups leveraging AI and machine learning. This expansion brings Google’s global engineering muscle and resource network straight to New Zealand’s tech hubs.

**Inspired by Kiwi Success**

While this is our first official combined Australia and New Zealand cohort, the expansion was inspired by two incredible Kiwi startups from last year’s programme:

- [Atomic Tessellator](https://atomictessellator.com/): This Auckland-based advanced materials simulation platform entered the program with a computational limitation of simulating a maximum of 44,000 atoms at once. Working alongside Google tech mentors, they completely shattered their previous record—scaling their infrastructure to simulate a maximum of 680,000 atoms. This technical breakthrough unlocked an entirely new scale of material modeling accuracy for their team.
- [ThroughLine](https://www.throughlinecare.com/): A social and mental care network that helps the internet safely respond to individuals experiencing emotional crises, ThroughLine utilises highly localised helpline routing and AI-powered support, drawing backing from angel networks across both New Zealand and Australia to build an impactful global safety solution.

**What Selected Founders Get**

Scaling a tech company from New Zealand requires global connections, specialised infrastructure, and expert advice. By joining this Trans-Tasman cohort, founders will get:

- **Direct AI Mentorship:** Work one-on-one with Google's AI experts to scale your infrastructure and solve architectural challenges.
- **Google Products & Infrastructure:** Get access to Google tools, frameworks, and cloud credits to build quickly and securely.
- **Trans-Tasman Collaboration:** Build lifelong bonds with an ambitious cohort of founders from across Australia and New Zealand, opening up regional and global pathways.
- **Strategic Masterclasses:** Practical sessions designed to sharpen product-market fit, leadership, and international go-to-market strategies.

**Shape the Future of Kiwi Tech**

Whether you’re using machine learning to protect natural ecosystems, optimise logistics, or redefine global SaaS, this program is built to scale your impact.

Applications are open until **July 19th**. We can't wait to see what you’re building.
