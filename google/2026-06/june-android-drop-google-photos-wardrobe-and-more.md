Last month, we announced a wave of exciting updates coming to Android, including Gemini Intelligence and more. We can’t wait for you to experience them. Today, we’re bringing you even more features for stronger safety, improved styling and easier sharing only on Android.

The latest Android updates help you get more from your favorite apps — whether you’re keeping your calls and family safer, searching an entire look in one go or planning an outfit in your digital wardrobe. Here’s a look at what’s new in the June Android Drop.

## 1\. Spot scammers impersonating your contacts.

Get peace of mind with alerts that spot suspected scammers impersonating your contacts. With [fake call detection](https://www.android.com/intl/en_us/new-features-on-android/featured/june-2026/?utm_source=blog&utm_medium=owned&utm_campaign=y26q2AndroidDrop#fake-call-detection), Phone by Google can now verify if a call is actually coming from your contact’s device. If a scammer pretends to call from a number you trust, you’ll receive a warning so you can end the call fast. Learn more about this feature, available on Android 12+ devices with Phone by Google, in our latest [Google Security blog post](https://blog.google/security/android-fake-call-detection).

Now available on all Android 14+ devices that have Circle to Search.

## 3\. Style the perfect outfit from your digital wardrobe.

Coming soon, Google Photos is giving you your own personal digital closet where you can mix and match outfits and try them on virtually. [Google Photos wardrobe](https://www.android.com/intl/en_us/new-features-on-android/featured/june-2026/?utm_source=blog&utm_medium=owned&utm_campaign=y26q2AndroidDrop#google-photos-wardrobe) effortlessly catalogs the clothes you are wearing in your photo library by turning them into snapshots you can browse on your phone — so it’s easier to get inspired, save your favorite looks and share them with friends. ## 4\. Enjoy extra peace of mind with Personal Safety app features for kids.

Feel confident as kids head out with safety features for the whole family — coming soon. Kids under 13 will have access to helpful features in the [Personal Safety app](https://www.android.com/intl/en_us/new-features-on-android/featured/june-2026/?utm_source=blog&utm_medium=owned&utm_campaign=y26q2AndroidDrop#personal-safety-app-for-kids), like displaying their medical information and setting emergency contacts on their device’s lock screen. They'll also be able to turn on car crash detection, which automatically calls emergency services and texts emergency contacts after an accident. Plus, teens can use Personal Safety app location features like Safety Check and real-time sharing with emergency contacts. The Personal Safety app is available globally.
